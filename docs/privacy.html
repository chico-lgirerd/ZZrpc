Privacy Policy — ZZrpc

Last updated: [DATE]

ZZrpc is a small, personal tool that shows the zig-zag.fm track you're currently listening to as your Discord Rich Presence. It has no backend server operated by the developer, no analytics, and no telemetry. This policy explains exactly what data the app touches and why.

What ZZrpc reads

  • The current track's public metadata (title, artist, album, cover art URL, duration) — looked up from zig-zag.fm's own public API, using the same login session your browser already has with zig-zag.fm.
  • Whether playback is playing or paused, and playback position — read from the zig-zag.fm page you already have open, to keep the Discord status accurate.

What ZZrpc sends, and to whom

  • The track metadata above is sent from your browser to a program running on your own computer (localhost) — it never leaves your machine over the network at this step.
  • That local program sends the track info to Discord, using Discord's own Social SDK and your own Discord account, to update your Rich Presence. This is the same kind of data any "currently playing" status shows — visible to people who can already see your Discord status, exactly like a music-app integration would show.

What ZZrpc stores

  • A Discord authorization token (access + refresh token), obtained via Discord's own OAuth2 login flow, is stored locally on your computer (~/.config/zzrpc/token.txt) so you aren't asked to log in again every time you start the app. It is never sent anywhere except back to Discord's own servers to refresh itself.
  • No other data is stored. There is no database, no account system, and no server operated by the ZZrpc developer that receives or retains any of your data.

Third parties

ZZrpc talks to two services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • zig-zag.fm — for track metadata, using your existing logged-in session in your own browser.
  • Discord — for authentication and Rich Presence, using your own Discord account and OAuth2 login.

The ZZrpc developer does not receive, store, or have access to any data exchanged between you, zig-zag.fm, and Discord.

Deleting your data

Since everything ZZrpc stores lives only on your own computer, you can delete it yourself at any time:

  • Delete ~/.config/zzrpc/token.txt to remove your stored Discord authorization — you'll be asked to log in again next run.
  • Revoke ZZrpc's access from your Discord Authorized Apps settings at any time.
  • Uninstall the userscript from your browser extension (e.g. Tampermonkey) to stop it reading zig-zag.fm data entirely.
